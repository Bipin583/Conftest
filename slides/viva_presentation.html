1. The CI/CD Bottleneck

  • Modern CI/CD pipelines require frequent automated test feedback.
  • Full regression suites take 45 to 180 minutes to run.
  • Causes severe developer idle wait-time and expensive cloud compute bills.

3. The Overconfidence Dilemma

Standard tree-based models output scores that do not match empirical failure frequencies.

Measured on our validation split: expected calibration error 0.0390 and worst-bin error 0.1235 before calibration.

Consequence: a confidence threshold set from raw scores does not mean what it appears to mean, so an abstention gate built on it cannot be trusted.

4. The ConfTest Solution

  1. 32-Feature Mining Pipeline (AST + Call-Graphs + History + Churn).
  2. 5-Seed Deep Ensemble for Epistemic Uncertainty ($\sigma$).
  3. Temperature Scaling at $T^* = 0.7941$, ECE $0.0390 o 0.0242$ (isotonic fitted and rejected: lower mean ECE, worst-bin error nearly doubled).
  4. Selective Policy with a full-suite fallback, which held zero escaped commits across 183 held-out commits by abstaining on 97.8% of them.
